### Executive Summary
The audit of the `mv_biomass_fermentation` dataset reveals significant structural integrity concerns that undermine the reliability of the bioconversion yields. Most critically, the dataset contains 123 duplicated rows and 126 instances of non-unique `record_id` values. In a high-throughput assay environment, the lack of unique identifiers prevents accurate tracking of replicates and can lead to overestimation of yields or incorrect mean calculations. Furthermore, the dataset suffers from a total loss of experimental context, as `experiment_id` and `experiment_date` columns are 100% null across all 2,996 records. This makes it impossible to correlate fermentation outcomes with specific temporal batches or to track calibration drift over time.

While core fermentation parameters like `strain_name` and `pretreatment_method` are well-populated, there are 246 records missing measurement units and 189 records missing provenance data (e.g., `provider_codename` and `collection_timestamp`). While the absence of `primary_product` for certain resources is expected per domain context, the missing units for approximately 8% of the data pose a significant risk to downstream analysis, particularly for titer and rate calculations where unit confusion (e.g., g/L vs. g/g) can lead to order-of-magnitude errors. Immediate remediation is required to restore experimental linkage and resolve the ID collisions.
