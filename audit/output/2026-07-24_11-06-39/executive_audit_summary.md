### Executive Summary
The audit of the `mv_biomass_composition` dataset, encompassing 6,366 records, identifies several critical data quality concerns that may impact the reliability of biomass characterization. The most significant finding is the presence of physically impossible negative values (minimum -244.0) and extreme outliers in the 'observed_value' column (maximum 161,000). These extremes, particularly in sugar yields (glucose_yld, xylose_yld) and moisture/solids parameters, suggest systemic issues with unit consistency, potentially involving a mix of percentage, mg/kg, and ppm scales without proper normalization. Furthermore, a total lack of 'experiment_date' metadata across the entire dataset hinders temporal trend analysis and instrument drift detection.

Anomalies are heavily clustered around almond-derived resources (shells, hulls, and branches) and grape pomace, which together account for a substantial portion of the 1,712 flagged observations. A notable pattern involves analyst 'xkang2@lbl.gov', who is associated with a high volume of low-severity flags in carbohydrate and lignin analyses for almond hulls and sargassum. Additionally, the discovery of a non-unique 'record_id' (6365 unique IDs for 6366 rows) indicates a primary key violation or data duplication error that must be resolved to ensure data integrity for downstream AIM1 modeling.
