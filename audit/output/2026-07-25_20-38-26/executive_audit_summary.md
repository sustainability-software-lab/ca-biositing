These issues collectively hinder the ability to validate bioconversion yields (e.g., Ethanol and 3HP titers) against expected physical limits, as the lack of temporal and vessel-specific context prevents the identification of calibration drift or contamination events. Priority should be given to restoring experimental IDs and resolving the record duplication to ensure the dataset's utility for downstream modeling.
