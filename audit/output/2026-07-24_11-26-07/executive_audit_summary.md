### Executive Summary
The audit of the `mv_biomass_fermentation` dataset reveals significant structural data integrity issues and substantial gaps in experimental metadata. The most critical finding is the 100% loss of traceability data, with `experiment_id`, `experiment_date`, and `analyst_email` appearing as entirely null across all 2,996 records. This lack of attribution prevents any temporal analysis or accountability for the fermentation runs. Furthermore, the presence of 123 duplicated rows and 126 non-unique `record_id` entries suggests a failure in the data ingestion pipeline or a join error in the materialized view creation.

From a domain perspective, the data quality is compromised by missing 'primary_product' and 'unit' fields for approximately 6-8% of the records. Without knowing the substrate (e.g., Almond Hulls vs. Corn Stover) or the measurement unit, calculating bioconversion yields (e.g., EtOH mol%) becomes scientifically impossible. Additionally, the 'observed_value' field shows a high frequency of zero values (15.72%), which, while expected for early-stage product titers, is concerning if distributed across T0 sugar concentrations. The lack of variation in 'reactor_vessel' and 'qc_pass' suggests this dataset may be a restricted or incorrectly filtered subset of a larger experimental campaign. Immediate reconciliation of the data pipeline and a re-import of missing metadata are required to make this dataset usable for research modeling.
