### Executive Summary
The audit of the `mv_biomass_gasification` dataset reveals significant data integrity and processing issues that impact the reliability of syngas composition analysis. Most critically, the dataset contains a high volume of duplicated rows (44) and is entirely missing temporal context, with 100% of the `experiment_date` and `primary_product` columns being null. This loss of temporal metadata prevents any trend analysis related to equipment drift or seasonal biomass variation. Furthermore, the statistical distribution of the `observed_value` has shifted noticeably from the reference period, with the median value dropping from 116.96 to 68.13, suggesting a change in the underlying sample population or reporting methods.

The most actionable finding involves the gasification of 'spent oak chips' and 'walnut shells' under the 'rigging' and 'energetic' providers. Notes provided by analyst iteran@ucmerced.edu explicitly state that several gas samples for oak chips require normalization (xi/sum(xi/xn)), indicating that the current 'observed_values' for major syngas components like H2, CO, and CH4 may be raw, non-compositional data. Additionally, walnut shell samples are flagged with notes indicating "poor gas sample" and inconsistencies between flammability limits and operational observations. These issues, combined with high variance in trace hydrocarbons (C4-C6), suggest that a significant portion of the current data batch should be treated as preliminary or compromised until normalization and data cleaning are completed.
