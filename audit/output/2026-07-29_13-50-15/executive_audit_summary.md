### Executive Summary
The data quality audit of the `mv_biomass_fermentation` dataset reveals several critical issues that impact the reliability of bioconversion performance metrics. Most significantly, the audit identified multiple instances of physically impossible yields exceeding 100% in olive-derived biomass (stems, leaves, and pomace) and lab media. These anomalies are explicitly noted in the records as potential underestimations of initial sugar concentrations or overestimations of product titer. Such discrepancies suggest a systemic calibration or calculation error in the fermentation mass balance, particularly for samples processed by analyst `mduran@lbl.gov`.

Furthermore, the dataset suffers from significant structural and metadata gaps. There is a 100% missing rate for `experiment_date`, which precludes temporal trend analysis, and approximately 8% of records are missing unit definitions. The presence of 246 duplicated rows and the absence of explicit parameter names in several flagged records further complicate the data's utility. Immediate corrective action is required to recalibrate the sugar-to-product conversion calculations and resolve the data duplication issues to ensure the integrity of the bioprocess research.
