## How it works
Per the PR body, the old hard-coded `_acreage_unit_id = 18` pointed at ’bushels’ and not ’acres’, so `census_acres` was NULL for every crop and zeroed acreage-based estimates in Paths B and D. The fix joins `Parameter` and `Unit` by `func.lower(name)`, and Path D’s coalesce becomes `_bnb_acres_expr`, then `_bearing_acres_expr`, then `_harvested_acres_expr`. The ’bushels’ versus ’acres’ ID claim is the PR’s and was not checked against the database.
